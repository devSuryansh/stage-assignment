EXT. VILLAGE THANA - VERANDA - MORNING


A low brick building. A faded board: पुलिस चौकी. Two plastic
chairs, one broken. A neem tree drops shade on a half-asleep
CONSTABLE. DARSHAN (45) arrives with a lathi of frustration
and a folded complaint paper.


                           DARSHAN
          बाबा जी, मेरी भैंस चोरी हो गई!


The Constable opens one eye, then the other, as if both
required separate effort.


                           CONSTABLE
          नाम?


                           DARSHAN
          मेरा या भैंस का?



INT. THANA - REPORT ROOM - DAY


A wooden desk. A ledger thicker than kindness. STATION
WRITER dips a pen. Darshan sits forward on a stool.
THANEDAR SAAB (50) enters with a brass tumbler of tea and
zero hurry.


                           THANEDAR
          भैंस का रंग?


                           DARSHAN
          काला। माथे पर सफ़ेद। गले में घंटी।


                           THANEDAR
          घंटी भी चोरी हो गई?


                           DARSHAN
          घंटी भैंस के गले में थी साब। भैंस गई तो घंटी कहाँ रहती?


The Writer writes slower, enjoying the sentence.



EXT. THANA COURTYARD - EVENING


A young man — GULAB (20) — is brought in by two villagers.
He looks at the ground. Darshan recognizes him and softens
despite himself.


                           DARSHAN
          ये तो मेरे भाई का लड़का है…


                           THANEDAR
          तो शिकायत वापस?


                           DARSHAN
          शिकायत नहीं… चाय पिला दो पहले। फिर पूछेंगे भैंस कहाँ है।
